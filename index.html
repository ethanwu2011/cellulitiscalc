    About CELTIC

    Every tier is logistic regression. The coefficients on this page are the model.

    Use it for

    A hospitalized adult with a red, warm, swollen limb, as a transparent estimate to set beside your examination.

    Do not use it for

    Children, outpatients, an obvious alternative diagnosis, necrotizing infection, or sepsis. It does not replace the examination, an ultrasound for suspected clot, or a dermatology consult. Never withhold antibiotics from an unstable patient because of it.

    Limits

    The reference standard was the coded discharge diagnosis, not expert adjudication. Discrimination was 0.70 at 70 years or older, the lowest of the age groups, where a mimic is most likely, so look twice at a rule-in in that group. No tier uses a culture result.

    The arithmetic, worked by hand

    logit = −1.076 + 1.530·unilateral + 0.187·leukocytosis − 0.654·age ≥ 70 − 0.562·malignancy + 0.399·diabetes
    probability = 1 / (1 + e−logit)

    Example: unilateral involvement, diabetes, age 74, no leukocytosis, no malignancy.

    logit = −1.076 + 1.530 + 0.399 − 0.654 = 0.199
    e−0.199 = 0.820
    probability = 1 / 1.820 = 0.549, shown as 55%, indeterminate

    Hand score: unilateral +3, diabetes +1, leukocytosis 0, age 70 or older −1, active malignancy −1. This patient totals +3 + 1 − 1 = +3. In the test set, 60% of patients with a hand score of +3 had cellulitis.

    The Rapid tier has the same form with 10 coefficients, printed in the source.
